Cryptid II

Building…

Two-player deduction on a tessellation board. Each player holds two secret clues; the habitat is the one cell that matches all four. Test a cell to place a marker: the opponent must rule it out with one of their clues if they can — and that clue becomes public. A marker the opponent cannot refute wins if it also matches your own clues; Search accuses a cell directly. Terrains are drawn from the board's own colour pattern. Best played with a screen between you.
